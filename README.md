# rccar

## Intro
- the purpose of this project was to challenge my cadding skills and see whether or not I could cad a series of complex mechanisms.
- at first, i wanted to cad an rccar with a center differential, front differential, rear differential, and steering with a servo, as well as 3DP suspensions. However, after a lot of struggle, I realized that this was unfeasible in the time span of 2 days. Instead, I opted for a car with a front differential and rear differential connected together by a 3mm rod with absolutely no steering.
- this made it so that my life was 100x easier since by the time i changed my mind, i had already cadded up the differential and gearbox, so all i had to do was cad the wheels and the mounts.
- however, there were still lots and lots of problems with my cad. because i didn't really do much sketching and planning before i went on to CADding, there were issues with where to place things and the fact that some things didn't fit (like the battery). there was also a way too small gap between the bottom of the chassis and the wheels, which i might be able to fix, but i didn't in fear that my entire cad is going to break.
- so, next steps: completely restart the CAD, and make it so that I have a clear mastersketch with all the dimensions I need and very parametric modeling. then, introduce steering and center differential.
- i definitely underestimated this project, but it did teach me a lot, so it wasn't too bad.
